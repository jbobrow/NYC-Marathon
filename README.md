# NYC Marathon Race Day

**Know where to be, and when.** A one-page race-day planner for the 2026 TCS New York City Marathon (Sunday, November 1).

Pick where you're watching — from a list of cross streets, by tapping the course map, or by pasting your coordinates — and the page works out when the wheelchair racers, the pros and the main field reach you, which subway gets you there, and where to go next. Turn on **Following a runner?** to add their wave and goal time and see when they'll reach your spot.

- Start times, corral times, TV coverage and road closures for race day (all times Eastern Standard Time; clocks fall back overnight)
- The 2026 course, including the new Bronx route between miles 20 and 21
- Estimates assume an even pace from the wave's start; use the TCS NYC Marathon app for live splits

Open `index.html` in a browser, or view it on GitHub Pages. No build step.

App by Jon Bobrow.
